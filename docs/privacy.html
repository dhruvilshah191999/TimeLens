TimeLens Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

TimeLens is a browser extension that shows how long you spend on websites and groups that time by intent (such as Work, Learning or Entertainment). It is built so that your browsing data never leaves your device.

Summary

  • All data is stored locally in your browser.
  • Nothing is sent to any server. There is no account, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Your data is never sold, shared or transferred to anyone.
  • You can export or delete your data at any time.

What the extension reads

To measure time and label it, TimeLens reads the URL and title of the tab you are actively using, and whether you are idle. It does not read page content, form inputs, passwords, cookies or anything typed into a page.

What is stored, and where

TimeLens stores the following in your browser's local extension storage (chrome.storage.local), on your device only:

  • Browsing sessions: the site, page title, start and end time, and the intent label for each session.
  • Daily totals of time per site and per intent.
  • Your settings, including any sites you have excluded from tracking.

Detailed sessions older than 90 days are deleted automatically. Daily totals are kept so long-term trends still work, until you delete them.

On-device AI labelling

On Chrome versions that include the built-in Prompt API, TimeLens can optionally use Chrome's on-device model (Gemini Nano) to label pages that the keyword rules cannot classify. This model runs entirely inside your browser. The page title and address are processed on your device and are not sent to Google, to the developer or to anyone else.

Network access

TimeLens makes no network requests. Fonts are bundled with the extension, and site icons are read from Chrome's local favicon cache.

Your control

  • Exclude sites: add sites in Settings, or right-click a page and choose "Stop tracking this site".
  • Export: download all of your data as a JSON file from Settings.
  • Delete: remove all of your data from Settings. Uninstalling the extension also deletes everything it stored.

Sharing

The developer has no access to your data, so it cannot be sold, shared, used for advertising, or used to determine creditworthiness or for lending.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised on the project's GitHub issues page.
